**An enormous sword. A kingdom made of candy. A director who wants another take.**

You look ready to end an age of darkness. Fenella needs you to get the rats to eat their celery.

Welcome to **The Dread Land of Candy**, a short single-player fantasy comedy about starring in a fairy director's increasingly questionable film. Walk through Sugarborough's candy scenery, pacify troublesome creatures, and try to preserve your dignity while Fenella handles direction, production, and the deductions from your pay.

When she calls **CUT!**, you get a say. Accept her rewrite, refuse it, or type a direction of your own. Ask for enormous rats. Demand a sadder scene. Make it about taxes. AI-assisted director's notes can change the next take's creatures, dialogue, mood, and difficulty—and help shape your film's premiere.

- **Choose your lead:** knight, mage, archer, or barbarian.
- **Shoot three increasingly troubled scenes:** rats with dietary objections, goblins in need of dental care, and an almond situation.
- **Put your skills to work:** pacify the cast, dodge charging nuisances, and get the take before your dignity runs out.
- **Exercise creative control:** follow Fenella's vision or give her something else to complain about.
- **Collect your cut:** earn loot and sprinkles, then inspect the producer's itemized deductions.

**Windows · Single-player · Game jam build**

Typed director's notes are sent to an online AI service. Offline, you can still play using the built-in notes and fallback directions.
