# B-roll index: Docs/Devlog/GameFootage (10 files)

Built Nov 15 from contact sheets (`Video/build/broll/*.jpg`, 1 frame per 5 s, or per 10 s for the two long files; timestamp burned into each tile). Files were not touched. Times are approximate to the sampling interval: scrub the edges at full speed. "Unconfirmed" means the sampled frames did not show it clearly.

Common notes for the six 1920x1080 capture runs (`*-run`, `village-quests`, `budget-out`, `cut-forcefail`, `title-idle`): no audio, no webcam overlay, all show the in-game HUD (objective bar, subtitle bubble from Fenella at bottom, ability bar bottom-left, "PAUSE / Esc" top right). They are autoplay bot runs, so the hero walks the yellow brick road on its own. Hero is small and seen from a high third-person camera, not a close-up. The 30-70 s stretch of every run is a pink cobbled hillside (Molar Hills / Fort Toothache) with the camera clipping through teal candy trees at times (blurred, 25-35 s and 60-65 s).
