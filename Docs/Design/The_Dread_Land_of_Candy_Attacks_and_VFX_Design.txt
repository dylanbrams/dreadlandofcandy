PURPOSE
=======
This document is self-contained. It inventories EVERY specifically named player attack/skill discussed in the game's prior brainstorming or written briefs, including superseded/alternative names, and turns the selected abilities into concrete effect-building briefs. It also describes reusable VFX systems, encounter reactions, the Director's Cut theme layer, voice variants, replication and a production-priority queue. You do not need to read the larger MMO design to start discussing effects.

Do not infer that any described ability, mesh, animation, voice clip, Niagara system, Blueprint, Gameplay Ability or network feature already exists. These are DESIGNS, not an implementation report. Some names are alternatives, not separate abilities. Avoid silently expanding a class's shippable skill count by treating every brainstorm as a commitment. Before modifying a real Unreal project, inspect its existing GAS setup, GameplayCue conventions, art and reference assets.

GAME PITCH AND CONTEXT
======================
Game: THE DREAD LAND OF CANDY: DIRECTOR'S CUT.
Village: SugarBorough.
Theme: 'Director's Cut'. An in-world Director of Heroic Affairs treats every ridiculous local errand as a magnificent fantasy epic, periodically shouting ACTION!, CUT! or RETAKE! and changing procedurally assembled quest stages. A Magic Candy Rock lets players contact quest NPCs remotely. No film-production simulation or live video editor is necessary.

Visual setup: the PLAYER characters are tall, imposing, grim dark-fantasy adventurers with weathered armor, genuine giant swords and apocalyptic casting poses. The WORLD is welcoming, bright, rounded, pastel and candy-saturated. Village NPCs treat these terrifying heroes as ordinary contractors. VFX bridge the styles: deeply serious anticipation, a wildly ridiculous payload (toothpaste, candy, celery, speech bubbles, rolling pins), monumental impact, and a harmless behavior change.

Gameplay: nonviolent action RPG / tiny co-op MMO-like vertical slice (not a real MMO server). Enemies/nuisances have RESOLVE rather than lethal health. When Resolve reaches zero, they become Pacified, Compliant or Dispersed. An E interaction may still be necessary (brush a goblin's teeth, offer celery to a rat). Real gameplay hits are authoritative; visuals are client-side decoration. Four classes: Mage, Archer, Knight, Barbarian. Default player level 7. All classes must be able to complete all quest objectives.

DESIGN STATUS KEY
=================
[CORE]     The preferred ability set from the combat companion (one basic + two heroes, plus a few optional picks). These are design choices, NOT confirmed implemented features.
[HERO]     Strong visual showcase and production priority.
[OPTION]   Previously proposed skill, not required for the jam; can share an effect or mechanic with a [CORE] skill.
[LEGACY]   Name from the original architecture brainstorming, possibly replaced later. Preserve for completeness, but don't automatically build it.
[MOTIF]    Visual example rather than a specified, independent playable ability.
[ENCOUNTER] NPC/environment action or contextual finishing interaction; not a player attack.
[DIRECTOR] Theme/quest presentation cue; not a damage ability.

'Formal Reprimand' = alternate name for 'Sword of Stern Instruction'.
'Rolling-Pin Spin' = early name for 'Rolling Pin Cyclone'.
'Ferocious Knead' = earlier name/near-equivalent of 'Furious Kneading'.
'Dental Floss Snare' = alternate form of 'Floss Snare'.
'Caramel Meteor' is best treated as an impact/presentation variant of 'Caramel Catastrophe', not an additional backend system.
'Frosting Nova' and 'Polite Intervention' could use one circle-AoE framework but are different character appearances.

II. THE FOUR MOST IMPORTANT EFFECTS — DETAILED SHOT LISTS
=========================================================
These four are the Friday showreel. If resources are limited, make ONE beautifully readable hero effect per class before attempting any optional attacks. Timing below is a PROPOSED starting point; measure against actual combat cadence and character animation.

K02 — SWORD OF STERN INSTRUCTION / FORMAL REPRIMAND
  Ability role: directional cone of Manners/Resolve, possibly brief Flustered/interrupt.
  Comedy: a horrifyingly serious knight unsheathes a huge sword as though about to end civilization... then does not swing it, but lectures enemies instead. This is a central visual joke; the sword fakeout MUST be legible.
  0.00–0.30s: Hero plants feet, reaches for huge sword. Glint, severe metal scrape, low orchestral/riser cue.
  0.30–0.65s: Sword rises to a big threatening pose. Freeze at apex. Hold silhouette just long enough for audience to expect a cleave.
  0.65–0.80s: Knight instead points sword like a teacher's pointer and takes an exaggerated breath. Music/impact anticipation momentarily drops.
  0.80–1.15s: Vocal frontal shock cone, translucent concentric air ribbons in royal blue and gold; giant angular speech bubble(s): 'ABSOLUTELY NOT.' / 'FORM A LINE!' / 'MIND YOUR TEETH!' / 'THIS IS A PUBLIC SPACE!'. No blade strike.
  1.15–1.55s: Papery punctuation scraps, heraldic geometric ripples, target wobble/flinch/WellBehaved pose.
  Tail: subtle fading word fragments and a disapproving 'tsk' or NPC gasp.
  Main preset: VFX.LectureCone + UI.ComicBubble.Jagged/Placard + SFX.SternLecture.
  Mechanic: server cone trace ONCE at the designated hit window, not once per drawn word. Bubble text is never a hitbox. Other players see the same animation and core event.
  Implementation shortcut: montage or procedural upper-body pose + Niagara cone mesh + 2D screen-facing widget. Keep camera at gameplay distance; cinematic closeup optional only for trailer.

B02 — ROLLING PIN CYCLONE
  Ability role: sustained radial AoE at bounded hit intervals, Domestic Resolve effect.
  Comedy: a grim berserker becomes a mobile kitchen cyclone of unrealistically many rolling pins.
  0.00–0.25s: 1–2 rolling pins orbit chest/waist, character braces and inhales; low wooden knock and rising flour puff.
  0.25–0.55s: spin accelerates, 4–8 visible mesh pins plus sprite/impostor silhouettes multiply; growing cream-colored flour cloud.
  0.55–1.65s: full whirlwind, MANY perceived pins despite small mesh budget. Thick flour funnel, distinct wooden rings, 'whup-whup' orbit rhythm; target contact flour/crumbs. Center remains legible to player.
  1.65–1.90s: finish, pins whip outward visually in a radial starburst, NO independent physically simulated damage.
  1.90–2.30s: debris falls, dust dissipates, possibly one leftover rolling pin rolls away and vanishes as a visual punchline.
  Main preset: VFX.FlourCyclone + mesh orbit component + SFX.WoodClackLoop.
  Mechanic: bounded server AoE tick (e.g. one hit per target per scheduled interval, not per mesh collision); cancel/interrupt behavior must stop effects cleanly.
  Performance: cap visibly animated 3D rolling pins around 20 at hero quality, using fewer on low settings and sprite silhouettes for the remainder. No replicated utensils as physics actors.

M02 — PEPPERMINT POPFALL
  Ability role: ground-targeted multi-wave Sweetness AoE.
  Comedy: an extremely grand high-magic summoning ceremony rains tiny peppermint candies.
  0.00–0.30s: imposing caster posture and ornate serious glyphs above target location; pink/red-white swirl.
  0.30–0.55s: peppermints wink into existence over marked circle, hovering for a ridiculous instant; candy 'tink' chimes.
  0.55–1.45s: staggered drops, clear impact pops on the ground, one small bouncy hop per selected mesh candy, small sugar cloud and striped afterimage.
  1.45–1.90s: final accent pop, several candies roll to stillness and dissolve, sugar sparkles fade.
  Main preset: VFX.PeppermintPop + decal/telegraph ring + SFX.CandyPopSequence.
  Mechanic: fixed number of authoritative area waves; one hit per target per wave, explicitly bounded. Individual falling candy meshes do not cause hits.
  Performance: about 12 candy bounce meshes maximum and sprite/shader duplicates for volume. Spawn candy debris locally; remove on pool timeout.

A02 — CELERY VOLLEY
  Ability role: 3–5 projectiles/spread, Nutrition/Resolve.
  Comedy: the archer launches vegetables with the precision and presentation of mythical armor-piercing arrows.
  0.00–0.15s: dead-serious bow nock/draw, slim emerald charge line; leaves curl around arrow rest.
  0.15–0.40s: rapid or simultaneous volley of clearly readable celery stalks with luminous green streaks and orange leaf flecks.
  0.40–0.70s: dry 'CRUNCH' on impact, leaf confetti and small jolting vegetable-green glyph; a few tiny stalk pieces tumble.
  Tail: target briefly shakes its head or glows ReluctantlyNourished.
  Main preset: VFX.CeleryVolley + SFX.CrispCrunch.
  Mechanic: authoritative projectile or spread traces; cap damage overlap. At least one projectile should be direct aim for reliable solo play.
  Performance: one celery mesh reused as projectile, simple trail emitter and leaf burst sprite. Make it readable at gameplay-camera distance.

DAMAGE/EFFECT FAMILIES
  Damage.Sweetness  — sticky candy, peppermint, caramel, gold/pink/red. Optional Sticky.
  Damage.Nutrition  — celery, carrot, broccoli; green and orange. Optional Healthy/ReluctantlyNourished.
  Damage.Hygiene    — foam, floss, mouthwash, soap; white/aqua/mint. Optional SqueakyClean.
  Damage.Manners    — shouts, lecture cones, banners, disapproval; royal blue/gold or vocal orange/red. Optional Flustered/WellBehaved.
  Damage.Domestic   — dough, rolling pins, whisk, flour, napkins; wood/cream/copper. Optional Dazed. This fifth family need not have a unique resistance category.

CORE FIVE-LAYER EFFECT GRAMMAR
  1. Anticipation: 0.1–0.5 sec, grand threatening fantasy gesture + visible telegraph.
  2. Main impact: clear <0.2s peak silhouette (foam, candy drops, speech wave, flour blast).
  3. Secondary debris: 0.2–1.0 sec, peppermint bounce, crumbs, paper confetti, napkin scrap.
  4. Status: brief persistent indicators while actual effect is live; Sticky strands, SqueakyClean shine, Flustered punctuation, etc.
  5. Afterglow: <3 sec ordinary cosmetic tail; keep it short enough for four-player overlap.

COMIC BUBBLE SYSTEM
  Jagged: barked commands/attacks. Phrases: 'DO BETTER!', 'EAT THE CELERY!', 'YOU CAN FIX THIS!', 'ABSOLUTELY NOT!', 'ENOUGH!', 'THIS IS A PUBLIC SPACE!'.
  Round: supportive/soft/rare exasperated voice. Phrases: 'You've got this.' / 'please just eat them'.
  Formal placard: knight/Director civic correction. Phrases: 'FORM A LINE', 'MIND YOUR TEETH', 'STAND PROPERLY', 'EXCUSE ME'.
  Suggested motion: pop in at ~0.5 scale, overshoot 1.15, settle 1.0; stagger bubbles at 0.12–0.25s; float and pop into paper confetti. Designate screen-space priority and anchor world position to actor or attack origin. Never cover health/Resolve/status display or quest tracker excessively.
  For repetitive abilities, avoid unreadable walls of text: around TWO legible bubbles per player simultaneously at most; remaining 'shouts' can be smaller punctuation-only particles. World UMG or facing sprite cards are fine.

VI. VOICE DESIGN — ELEVENLABS / RANDOM LANGUAGE / PARENT VARIANT
===============================================================
Concept established in conversation: 'EAT YOUR VEGETABLES!' is a disproportionately epic multilingual barbarian battle cry. Every recorded language must convey the SAME meaning rather than inventing different threats. Localize the on-screen comic bubble into the player's UI language (English initial target), keeping the joke comprehensible. The fun is the wildly serious delivery versus the mundane instruction, not making a joke of a particular nationality/accent.

Voice production: PRE-GENERATE, LICENSE/ATTRIBUTE AS REQUIRED, and package short clips. Do NOT call a TTS API with every attack; that increases latency, cost, dependency and desync. ElevenLabs is an option, not guaranteed available or required. Verify pronunciation/translation with reliable sources or a competent speaker; don't rely on one model's unsupported phonetics. Obtain necessary rights/consents for voice characteristics; avoid copying recognizable actors.

Performance tiers (proposed):
  SHOUT_EPIC: booming fantasy battle command in chosen language; loud full delivery. Primary B03 variant.
  SHOUT_PARENT: a defeated, deeply exasperated parent, quieter, conversational, emotionally spent: 'Please. Just eat them.' This is a specifically requested FUNNIEST RARE LINE. Present as an unheroic anticlimax with tiny lowercase/rounded bubble 'please just eat them' while the enormous underlying skill VFX continues without changing power.
  SHOUT_DIRECTOR_RETAKE: Director yells 'CUT! We need an international version!' and the next use of the shout plays a different language; optional, script-only.

Randomization: choose from a small deterministic/local table of known clip IDs at activation. The previous conversation floated 25% English, 65% non-English and 10% overdramatic extended delivery as EXPERIMENTAL weights, not a locked requirement. The exasperated parent variant was suggested at roughly 3% on a qualifying critical attack, but its actual frequency should be adjusted for comedic timing and clip availability. Avoid firing multiple long overlapping voice clips in co-op. Replicate/publish voice_variant_id for consistent shared timing if needed; the server still owns only the gameplay result.

Candidate English captions:
  'EAT YOUR VEGETABLES!'
  'EAT THE CELERY!'
  'DO BETTER!'
  'YOU CAN FIX THIS!'
  Rare caption: 'please just eat them'

Audio layering: theatrical inhale / controlled silence / roar / low-frequency fantasy impact; 2D paper pops on comic bubbles; target reaction squeak. For SHOUT_PARENT, sharply drop the heroic voice processing and let the embarrassing domestic exhaustion land. The ability deals the same authoritative Resolve/status regardless of voice clip.

VII. DIRECTOR'S CUT — PRESENTATION EFFECTS, NOT A NEW DAMAGE SYSTEM
=================================================================
The actual jam theme is 'Director's Cut'. Quests are called Scenes with numbered Takes. An NPC/voice called the Director of Heroic Affairs may request a new ending or alternate compatible quest stage. All changes must be selected from prebuilt tested objective templates; do not spawn LLM-authored mechanics.

D01 [DIRECTOR] ACTION!   | clapperboard clap; 'SCENE 03 / TAKE 02' title; short lens flare/spotlight; start quest stage.
D02 [DIRECTOR] CUT!      | giant film-slate / comic CUT overlay; short snap/silence, then choice of alternate objective; not an interrupt of arbitrary combat frames.

Keep overlays short, minimize camera theft, and do not require an actual film editor, camera crew, or replay system. An additional joke: this is 'The Definitive Edition of a Game That Never Existed.'

VIII. NPC / QUEST PAYOFF EFFECTS (CRUCIAL TO THE NONVIOLENT JOKE)
=================================================================
Reducing Resolve to zero is NOT always quest completion. There should be an explicit contextual action and satisfying post-action image; don't use conventional death or corpse effects.

RATS + CELERY:
  Rat stops crop eating, sits with offended expression. The player presses E or another assigned interact to OFFER celery. Short leafy flourish, slow reluctant crunch, green success sparkles. Crit from Nutrition can accentuate chewing but must not replace interact if quest requires it.

KIDS + CROPS:
  Child or group stops running through rows because of conversation/attention, NOT because of being hurt. A magical line/path appears on soil, kids tiptoe in an orderly procession, little politely straightened posture or queue placards. Use dialogue/escort/interact, not physical attack damage on kids.

MINE / CONDENSED TREACLE:
  Serious dungeon reveal; dark rocks with ridiculous syrup stalactites. Extracting syrup triggers gold viscous strands, heroic jar-seal stamp, tiny glistening drops. Could use caramel splash shader without fluid simulation.

LOLLYPOP BUGS + TAMED ALMONDS:
  Bugs zip and distract almonds; upon pacification, bugs spiral upward and vanish in candy glitter. Almonds stop wobbling and begin obediently gathering, sleepy twinkle. Avoid lifelike insect gore.

FORT TOOTHACHE:
  Goblin at zero Resolve opens mouth in extreme reluctant cooperation. E interaction animates oversized toothbrush scrub and enormous FOAM BURST. End with 'SQUEAKY CLEAN' dental sparkle/star glint and proud/surprised goblin face. For boss Supreme Plaque Warden, add bigger bubble waves and dramatic oversized toothbrush finish. A single room, 3 goblins, one special enemy is enough for the jam.

IX. TECHNICAL EFFECTS ARCHITECTURE FOR UNREAL 5.8 / GAS
======================================================
Assume an existing Unreal 5.8 game and considerable local C++/Blueprint/GAS reference code. INSPECT it before designing new interfaces. Exact UCLASS names, GameplayCue tags, assets, and plugins below are naming proposals rather than claims about the project.

Separation of concerns:
  A. Skill definition: data for input slot, class, cooldown, energy, geometry, gameplay effect, status, VFX preset ID, animation, SFX and cosmetic choice.
  B. Authoritative executor: validate cast, cooldown/range/targets, apply Resolve and status effects, update quest objective events exactly once.
  C. Cosmetic activation event / GameplayCue: ability_id, caster actor, aim point/direction, server cast timestamp, random_seed, voice_variant_id, scale_tier and optionally hit list. Clients drive visuals; do NOT replicate each particle/rolling pin.
  D. Ability VFX actor/component: spawns or plays Niagara/mesh attachments, timed UMG popups, one-shot/loop audio, camera impulses, status sprites and cleanup.
  E. Quality tiers: lower debris counts and reduce translucency, preserve anticipation + impact silhouette and readable sound.

Suggested common ability fields:
  ability_id, class_id, display_name, status[CORE|OPTION|LEGACY], damage_tag,
  geometry[projectile|cone|radial|ground_aoe|melee_arc|self_buff|interact],
  range_m, radius_m, cone_angle_deg, windup_ms, active_ms, recovery_ms,
  cooldown_s, max_targets, damage_per_authoritative_hit, num_waves,
  status_tag, status_duration_s, animation_cue, vfx_preset_id,
  sfx_cue_id, bubble_style_id, voice_pool_id, effect_quality_scalars.

Example (illustrative only, not a verified API contract):
  ability_id: B03
  name: Aggressive Encouragement
  geometry: cone
  damage_tag: Damage.Manners
  vfx_preset_id: VFX.LectureCone
  bubble_style_id: UI.ComicBubble.Jagged
  voice_pool_id: Voice.EatYourVegetables.Multilingual
  animation_cue: Anim.Barb.VocalShout
  gameplay_hit: server cone query at animation notify
  client_only: comic text, waves, audio, camera impulse

Timing and multiplayer:
  - Predict local windup only if the current GAS implementation supports it reliably.
  - Cast/target validation and impacts are host authoritative (or dedicated server if infrastructure changes later).
  - Cosmetic sequencers may locally spawn MANY objects but the host sends ONE compact activation/cue; do not replicate dozens of rolling pins or peppermint rigidbodies.
  - Actor spawn/destroy/pacified state must replicate; transient VFX need not persist for joining players.
  - If an ability is interrupted, end looping sound/mesh orbit emitter and restore animation state; no stuck whirlwinds or speech balloons.
  - Stacking/cooldowns/Resolve should be tested with 2 players hitting the same target.
  - Do not trigger an ElevenLabs HTTP request while a combat montage waits.

Effects and assets (suggested): Niagara for foam/dust/candy sprites/sound-wave mesh particles; Static Mesh for 1 representative peppermint/stalk/rolling pin/toothbrush; UMG world-space widget or camera-facing sprites for comic bubbles; pooled/short-lived decals for caramel/frosting/flour. Meshes are cosmetic unless the server's ability execution explicitly uses a corresponding gameplay projectile. A single shared material function for palette controls will help cohesion.

Performance baseline (initial proposal; profile, don't treat as guaranteed):
  - <= ~20 visible animated 3D rolling pins during ONE hero cast, fewer at low quality; fake more with cards.
  - <= ~12 bouncing peppermint meshes at once; add sprites for the impression of many.
  - <= 2 readable comic speech bubbles per player on screen at once; additional punctuation particles fine.
  - Ordinary cosmetic tail under ~3s; status duration can be longer.
  - Limit local camera shake, transparency, overdraw and simultaneous one-shot audio.
  - For 2–4 players, make effects legible under overlap; preserve enemy silhouettes and quest HUD.
  - Expose reduced flashes/screenshake/particles options where feasible.

Visual cohesion: the grim heroes, pastel world, and comedy particles must share camera, lighting and exposure. Pink world lights should bounce/reflection on dark armor. Avoid copied proprietary armor/characters. No blood, lethal finishers, or grotesque fear framing.
